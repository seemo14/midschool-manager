ℹ️ About

MidSchool Manager v1.0.0

A class management app for Moroccan middle school (collège) teachers.

All data is stored locally in your browser — nothing is sent to any server.

Features: Lesson Records, Materials Tracker, Lesson Plans, Calendar, Student Management, Assessments, Remedial Work Tracking.
